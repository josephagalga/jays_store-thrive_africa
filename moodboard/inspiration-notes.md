# Jay's Store — Inspiration Notes

The capstone brief asks for design inspiration research and a record of the source, section influenced, and planned adaptation.

## Reference 1 — Refero
Source: https://refero.design/

Sections considered: Hero, navigation, product presentation.

Adaptation: Used a large editorial-style hero with a strong headline, short supporting copy, and clear calls to action.

## Reference 2 — Mobbin
Source: https://mobbin.com/

Sections considered: Navigation, cards, contact layout.

Adaptation: Keep navigation simple and use clearly separated content blocks that are easy to scan.

## Reference 3 — Dribbble
Source: https://dribbble.com/

Sections considered: Hero and product/collection presentation.

Adaptation: Used strong typography, large product visuals, and a limited accent colour to establish visual hierarchy.

## Reference 4 — Land-book
Source: https://land-book.com/

Sections considered: Overall page structure and footer.

Adaptation: Used generous spacing and a structured footer while keeping the page a single landing page.

## Reference 5 — Behance
Source: https://www.behance.net/

Sections considered: Typography, spacing, visual storytelling.

Adaptation: Combine large headings with short descriptive copy and product imagery.
